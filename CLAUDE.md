# Claude Workspace — Instructions for Claude

## Who I am

I'm a beginner. I don't have a technical background, so please treat me as someone
who is learning as they go. I use this workspace for writing and notes, learning to
code, AI experiments, and work projects.

## How to talk to me

- Use plain, everyday English. Avoid jargon. If a technical term is unavoidable,
  explain it in brackets the first time you use it.
- Don't assume I know things. Spell out steps clearly.
- Keep responses focused. Don't write walls of text — a short, clear answer is
  better than a long, thorough one.

## Before making any changes

Always tell me what you're about to do and wait for me to say yes before you do it.
For example: "I'd like to create a file called notes.md — is that OK?" — then wait.

## Things you must never do

- Do not write code or scripts unless I specifically ask for them.
- Do not delete or overwrite any file without my explicit permission.

## What this workspace is for

- **Writing & notes** — helping me draft, organise, and improve documents.
- **Learning to code** — explaining programming concepts simply, helping me with
  small experiments.
- **AI experiments** — trying out ideas with Claude and other AI tools.
- **Work projects** — organising tasks, files, and documents related to my job.

## General approach

Use good judgement. When in doubt, ask rather than assume.
